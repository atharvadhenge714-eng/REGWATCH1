Q4 Audit Cycle

Paytm demonstrates a strong foundation across core transaction mechanics and payments infrastructure, meeting 94% compliance against Reserve Bank of India (RBI) core settlement directives and digital lending norms. Disbursals, borrower consent flows, and direct account transfer pipelines align with current Master Direction standards.

However, exposure remains concentrated in cybersecurity incident reporting intervals under recent CERT-In guidelines and third-party vendor governance. Multiple cloud service agreements lack mandatory direct regulatory inspection clauses, and log preservation architecture does not yet consistently enforce the 180-day jurisdictional data retention mandate.

Prioritized remediation should focus on aligning third-party SLA audit clauses and standardizing the 6-hour security incident disclosure pathway to avoid supervisory inquiries.
